## Before you send anything

- Fill in `[Name]`, `[Phone]`, `[Site]` and `[demo link]`.
- **Add a sign-off with your business name and a way to opt out.** Under the Spam Act 2003,
  commercial electronic messages need sender identification and a functional unsubscribe.
  Cold B2B to a published business address generally works on inferred consent — but the
  identification and unsubscribe parts aren't optional. Get your own advice if you're unsure.
- Send from a real domain, not gmail. These people check.

## Rules that apply to all six

1. **Never benchmark $20k against a monthly SaaS fee.** Benchmark against the admin hire they're
   about to make, or against years of subscription they'll never own. You lose the first comparison
   every time.
2. **Lead with their words, not your features.** Every hook in this pack is a quote from their own
   site. That's why they land.
3. **Ask one question, then stop typing.** These are short on purpose. A tradie who opens a
   six-paragraph email closes it.
4. **Don't cross-reference prospects.** Patricia (APC) sits on the AWCI SA Committee and will know
   the Curyers at Master Linings. Keep the conversations separate.
5. **Two are phone-only** — Compliant Fire and ICS publish no email address at all. Don't wait for
   a channel that doesn't exist.
