## Phone script

**Open:** "Hi, I'm after whoever looks after the office side. … My name's [X], I build custom software for trade businesses. Two minutes?"

**The hook:**

> "Your site says you test and certify every scaffold you build to AS 1576, and issue JSAs and SWMS per job. If a regulator rang this afternoon and asked for the certificate on a scaffold you put up eight months ago — how long would that take to find?"

**Second question, the real one:**

> "And how do you know what stock is on which site right now, and when it's due back?"

That second one is the actual product. Every scaffolder has this problem and nobody sells the answer.

---

## Why generic software fails here

Scaffold isn't jobs. It's:

- **Stock allocation** — which bays, boards and tubes are where
- **Erect date + dismantle date + hire period** running per structure
- **A handover certificate** per scaffold, per variation
- **Re-inspection intervals** while it stands

ServiceM8 models exactly none of this. Simpro does hire, but not scaffold-shaped hire.

If ICS is losing stock — and every scaffolder is — the software pays for itself in recovered gear before it saves a minute of admin. **Lead with the stock, not the paperwork.**
