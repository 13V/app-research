## Why this one can only be built

There is **no off-the-shelf product** that produces a doorset-level ITP pack against a hardware schedule. Not ServiceM8, not Tradify, not Fergus. Simpro does generic ITPs, badly, at enterprise cost.

Add to that:
- **Fire door compliance** — tagging and certification per leaf
- **FF&E tracking** — a different beast to job costing
- **Live-environment work** — Modbury was delivered inside a running hospital with staged access

Every one of those is a custom field structure. This is the cleanest $20k justification on the list.
