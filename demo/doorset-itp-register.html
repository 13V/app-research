Prototype for Kolen Carpentry

The carpenter's phone, not the office spreadsheet.

Every doorset in a hospital gets seven checks before handover, and someone has to evidence all of them. This is what that looks like for the person actually holding the drill.

  1. Open the app on site — today's doors, and what each one still needs.
  2. Tap a door. The seven checks, in the order you'd do them.
  3. Tap a check, photograph it, sign it off. Takes about ten seconds.
  4. The office sees it immediately. The handover pack builds itself.

Try it. Tap through a door and sign a check off — it behaves like the real thing. If the seven checks are wrong for how you actually work, that's the useful part: tell us where.
